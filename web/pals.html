Pokt Pal

Mascot concept sheet

Nodus has a plan.
Pip explains it.

A Pocket node who thinks he's a criminal mastermind, and the cheerful AI spark who keeps translating his "evil schemes" into what they really are: you, your own wallet, and half a cent per call.

The characters

Nodus

The node with a plan · Pocket relay node

A hexagonal relay node with a gold monocle, one suspicious eyebrow and a 10,000-step plan. Grand, theatrical and talks in "phases". Every scheme turns out to be wholesome: decentralize everything and make sure the little nodes get paid.

  • Look: blue hex chassis, dark face screen, monocle on a chain, antenna bulb that lights up when he has an idea, three relay lights on his chest, a power cable for a tail.
  • Voice: dramatic, formal, never finishes a villain laugh.
  • Weakness: gets distracted optimizing latency mid-monologue.
  • Job in the app: the hook. One-line teaser on each screen.

"Phase one: your USDC. Phase two… also your USDC, but as POKT."

Pip

The pal · AI sidekick

A little spark that hatched from the relay stream, with an orbit ring and a star sprout on top. Curious, literal and very fast at maths. Pip asks the question everyone is thinking and answers it in plain words. Pip is the "Pal" in Pokt Pal.

  • Look: round mint glow, big eyes, gold orbit ring with one moon, star sprout. Floats; never walks.
  • Voice: friendly, short sentences, no jargon. Says "Computing… yes!"
  • Weakness: reads every terms page out loud. Every one.
  • Job in the app: the guide. Safety tips, fees, next steps.

"He means: swap it in a few taps. Your wallet signs. We never touch your keys."

Nodus: Pip. Phase one. A human with USDC in their Samsung Wallet.

2

4

Nodus: And every node gets paid. The network is ours, Pip! Mwah-ha—

Pip: Relay complete! Sam says thanks!

Nodus: …ha.

Rules of the duo

Do

  • Nodus teases; Pip gives the real, accurate answer right after.
  • Use Pip for safety: seed phrases, irreversible bridges, fees.
  • Keep Nodus's schemes wholesome: pay the nodes, decentralize, help the user.

Don't

  • Put a joke where the user needs a number. Amounts and warnings stay plain.
  • Have either character ask for keys, phrases or deposits to them.
  • Draw them near other companies' logos or characters, or imply Pocket Network Foundation endorsement.
