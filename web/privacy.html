Pokt Pal

Privacy policy

Applies to the Pokt Pal web app and the Pokt Pal Android app. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Pokt Pal is non-custodial software. It helps you build transactions that you sign in your own wallet app. We never see, store or ask for your seed phrase or private keys, and we never hold your funds.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or advertising IDs.
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera access.

What our server handles

When you ask for a quote, a swap, a stake or a bridge route, the app sends our server the public wallet address, token and amount needed to prepare that transaction. These are public blockchain details, not secrets. Our server uses them to answer the request and does not keep them in a database. Like any web server, the hosting infrastructure processes your IP address to deliver the response, and may keep short-lived error logs for troubleshooting.

Transactions you sign are public on the Solana and Pocket blockchains. That is how blockchains work and is outside our control.

Services the app talks to

To work, the app contacts these third parties directly or through our server. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Your wallet apps (for example Phantom, Solflare, Keplr), which sign transactions.
  • Jupiter, for swap quotes and swap transactions on Solana.
  • Pocket Network and Keplr public RPC endpoints, and Solana RPC, to read balances and send signed transactions.
  • WalletConnect (Reown) relay, only if you connect Keplr Mobile, to pass messages between this app and your wallet.
  • jsDelivr, a content delivery network that serves open-source code libraries the app loads.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps small settings on your device, such as your WalletConnect pairing, so you stay connected. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes them.

Children

Pokt Pal is not directed at children and is meant for adults who may use crypto assets where they live.

Deleting your data

Because we keep no account or personal profile, there is nothing for you to delete with us. To remove local data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if what the app does changes. Questions: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
