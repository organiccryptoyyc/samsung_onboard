## Full description (4000 max)

```
Meet Nodus and Pip, your guides to Pocket Network.

Nodus is a scheming genius of a Pocket node. Pip is his cheerful AI sidekick who gives you the plain answer. Together they walk you from USDC in your wallet to POKT, step by step.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Buy POKT with USDC on Solana, or sell it back to USDC
• Swap other Solana tokens to and from POKT
• Get a little SOL for network fees in one tap
• Bridge to native POKT on Pocket Network
• Stake native POKT with Keplr
• Browse the Agent Marketplace: AI-ready services on Pocket (blockchain RPC, web search, SEC filings, clinical trials, sanctions screening and more) at half a cent a call
• Connect Pocket's tools to your AI agents with MCP and API links
• See every chain Pocket serves

YOUR KEYS STAY YOURS
Pokt Pal is non-custodial. It never asks for your seed phrase, never holds your funds, and never signs for you. Every transaction opens in your own wallet app (Phantom, Solflare or Keplr) for you to review and approve.

NO ACCOUNT, NO TRACKING
No sign-up, no ads, no analytics.

GOOD TO KNOW
• You need a Solana wallet app (Phantom or Solflare) and, for staking, Keplr.
• Swaps are routed by Jupiter. Prices move, and network fees apply.
• Crypto assets are volatile and can lose value. Nothing in this app is financial advice.

Pokt Pal is independent software. It is not affiliated with Samsung, Solana Foundation or Pocket Network Foundation.
```
